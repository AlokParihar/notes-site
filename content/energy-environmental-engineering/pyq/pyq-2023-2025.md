## Exam pattern (consistent across all papers)

- **Total questions:** 8 (Q1 compulsory-style, Q2–Q8 pick any 5 of 7 remaining... actually structured as attempt any 5 of 8 total)
- **Marks:** 70, Time: 3 hours
- **Format:** every question bilingual (English + Hindi), each worth equal marks
- **Q8 pattern:** almost always "write short notes on any two of the following" — a grab-bag of smaller topics

## Topic frequency across 5 papers (Dec 2023 → Dec 2025)

| Topic | Appears in |
|---|---|
| Biodiversity (definition, levels, hotspots, conservation) | Dec 2025, June 2025, Dec 2024, Dec 2023 |
| Energy systems / renewable alternatives / efficiency | Dec 2025, June 2025, Dec 2024, Dec 2023 |
| Ecosystems (structure, function, energy flow, food chains) | Dec 2025, June 2025, Dec 2024 |
| Pollution (air, water, solid waste) | Dec 2025, June 2025, Dec 2024, Dec 2023 |
| Climate change / global warming | Dec 2025, Dec 2023 |
| Legislation (Wildlife Protection Act, Forest Conservation Act, etc.) | Dec 2025, Dec 2024, Dec 2023 |
| Short notes grab-bag (Q8) | All 5 papers |

**Takeaway:** biodiversity, energy alternatives, and pollution are the safest bets — they show up in nearly every paper. Legislation-based short notes (Q8) are also a near-guaranteed easy pick if you memorize act names and their purpose in one line each.

## December 2025

**Q1.** (a) Explain the concept of energy systems and discuss major energy resources. (b) Discuss sustainability and environmental trade-offs of fossil fuel-based energy systems.

**Q2.** (a) Describe renewable energy alternatives to fossil fuels with examples. (b) Explain energy storage technologies such as pumped storage and high-efficiency batteries.

**Q3.** (a) Define an ecosystem and explain its structure and functions. (b) Describe energy flow and ecological pyramids in an ecosystem.

**Q4.** (a) Explain forest and grassland ecosystems highlighting their characteristics. (b) Discuss aquatic ecosystems with reference to ponds and oceans.

**Q5.** (a) Define biodiversity and explain its different levels. (b) Discuss India as a mega-diversity nation and biodiversity hotspots.

**Q6.** (a) What are the major threats to biodiversity in India? (b) Explain in-situ and ex-situ conservation methods.

**Q7.** (a) Define environmental pollution and discuss causes and effects of air and water pollution. (b) Explain solid waste management and the role of individuals in pollution prevention.

**Q8.** (a) Discuss climate change and global warming with suitable examples. (b) Explain major environmental protection acts in India and public awareness.

## June 2025

**Q1.** (a) Describe the different stages involved in the extraction, processing and utilization of coal as a fossil fuel. (b) What are the environmental impacts associated with each stage?

**Q2.** (a) Compare and contrast the different types of hydro energy technology, applications, advantages, limitations. (b) Illustrate the concept of energy sustainability and its relationship with environmental, social and economic development.

**Q3.** (a) Describe the structure and function of a forest ecosystem, including the role of producers, consumers, decomposers. (b) How does human activity impact forest ecosystems, and consequences on biodiversity and ecosystem services?

**Q4.** (a) Explain in brief about the energy flow and nutrient cycling in an aquatic ecosystem. (b) Mention the importance of community involvement in biodiversity conservation efforts in India.

**Q5.** (a) Explain the concept of hotspots of biodiversity — different hotspots of biodiversity in India. (b) Describe the role of India in global biodiversity conservation efforts.

**Q6.** (a) Describe the causes, effects, and control measures of air pollution in urban areas. How can individuals contribute to reducing air pollution? (b) Explain in brief about the government's responsibilities in mitigating the air pollution issue in urban areas.

**Q7.** (a) Discuss the impact of urbanization on the environment. (b) Describe the different strategies for mitigating the environmental impacts of urbanization.

**Q8.** (Short notes) Forest Conservation Act 1980 · Rainwater harvesting · Genetic diversity · Hydrogen energy — explain any two briefly.

## December 2024

**Q1.** (a) Describe the concept of energy efficiency and its importance in reducing energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions. (b) Discuss the various strategies and technologies used to improve energy efficiency in buildings, industries and transportation sectors.

**Q2.** (a) State the advantages and disadvantages of using nuclear energy as a source of electricity. (b) Describe in brief about the environmental impacts of fossil fuel combustion and greenhouse gas emissions on air pollution, water pollution.

**Q3.** (a) Discuss the importance of conservation and management of ecosystems for maintaining biodiversity and ecosystem services. (b) Mention the different types of approaches and strategies for ecosystem conservation and management.

**Q4.** (a) Explain the concept of ecological pyramids and also describe its various types. (b) Discuss in brief about which local communities can contribute to conservation, including ecotourism, sustainable livelihoods and traditional knowledge with a suitable example.

**Q5.** (a) Describe the importance of biodiversity at global, national and local region. (b) How come the human involvement impact biodiversity at each level, and state its consequences for ecosystem services and human well-being.

**Q6.** (a) Describe the causes and effects of marine pollution including the impact on human health and the environment. (b) Discuss the impact of nuclear hazards on human health and the environment.

**Q7.** (a) Describe the concept of Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and its importance in promoting sustainable development. (b) Discuss the different stages of the EIA process.

**Q8.** (Short notes) Wildlife Protection Act 1972 · Watershed management · Species diversity · High-efficiency batteries — explain any two briefly.

## June 2024

**Q1.** How is an ecosystem evolved? Write different components of an ecosystem. How is balance maintained in an ecosystem? Comment on the statement, "An ecosystem with large population is not healthy."

**Q2.** (a) What is Biodiversity? Discuss about the different kinds of diversity in organisms. Write in detail about conservation of biodiversity. (b) Give the classification of hydro-electric power plant. What are the criteria of site selection for hydroelectric plant?

**Q3.** (a) Differentiate between single-basin and double-basin tidal plants. Explain briefly the various components of tidal power plants. (b) Explain the term Solid waste. What are adverse effects of solid wastes in society? Discuss various methods for disposal of solid wastes.

**Q4.** Write short notes on any two: Fossil fuels · Ozone layer depletion · Acid rain.

**Q5.** (a) What is Food Chain? Explain various types of food chains. (b) Explain aquatic Ecosystem.

**Q6.** (a) What do you understand by concept of Green building? Explain. (b) What is Energy conservation? Write down different methods of energy conservation.

**Q7.** Write brief notes on any two: Ecological pyramid · Ethics · Global warming · Disaster management.

**Q8.** Write brief notes (Hindi section) on any two: Ecological pyramid · Policy · Global warming · Disaster management.
