## How to read this page

- Built only from the 5 papers on the Previous Year Papers page. No question here is invented or predicted.
- **Papers** = in how many of the 5 papers a question on that topic was asked. A short-note option counts, even though you may skip it in the exam.
- Five papers is a small sample. This shows what has been asked often, not what will come.
- This subject rarely repeats a question word for word. The same topics come back in new wording, so learn the topic, not one fixed answer.
- Modules follow the Syllabus page. For the mid-sem, ask your faculty which modules it covers and use only those sections.
- Question numbers (like Dec 2025 Q7(a)) match the Previous Year Papers page, so you can check any line there.
