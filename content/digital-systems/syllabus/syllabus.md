## Unit 1 — Boolean Algebra & Simplification

- Review of number systems and number base conversions
- Binary codes, Boolean algebra, Boolean functions, logic gates
- Simplification of Boolean functions — Karnaugh map methods, SOP-POS simplification, NAND-NOR implementation

## Unit 2 — Combinational Logic

- Half adder, half subtractor, full adder, full subtractor
- Look-ahead carry generator, BCD adder, series and parallel addition
- Multiplexer, demultiplexer, encoder, decoder, arithmetic circuits, ALU

## Unit 3 — Sequential Logic & Semiconductor Memories

- Flip-flops: D, T, S-R, J-K, master-slave, racing condition, edge & level triggered circuits
- Shift registers; asynchronous and synchronous counters — types and state diagrams
- Semiconductor memories; digital ICs (2716, 2732, etc.) and address decoding
- Modern trends: DRAM, FLASH RAM, etc.; designing with ROM and PLA

## Unit 4 — Converters, Multivibrators & Logic Families

- A/D & D/A converters and types; sample and hold circuits
- Voltage-to-frequency and frequency-to-voltage conversion
- Multivibrators: bistable, monostable, astable, Schmitt trigger, IC 555 and applications
- TTL, PMOS, CMOS, NMOS logic; interfacing TTL to MOS

## Unit 5 — Digital Communication

- Nyquist sampling theorem; time division multiplexing; PCM; quantization error
- Introduction to BPSK & BFSK modulation schemes
- Shannon's theorem for channel capacity
