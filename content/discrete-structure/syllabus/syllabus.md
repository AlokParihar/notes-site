## Unit 1 — Set Theory, Relation, Function, Theorem Proving Techniques

- **Set Theory:** countable/uncountable sets, Venn diagrams, proofs of general identities
- **Relation:** definition, types, composition, pictorial representation, equivalence relation, partial ordering, job-scheduling problem
- **Function:** one-to-one, into, onto, inverse, composition, recursively defined functions, pigeonhole principle
- **Theorem proving:** mathematical induction, proof by contradiction

## Unit 2 — Algebraic Structures

- Definition, properties, types of algebraic structures
- Semigroups, Monoids, Groups, Abelian groups, properties of groups
- Subgroups, cyclic groups, cosets, factor groups, permutation groups, normal subgroups
- Homomorphism and isomorphism of groups — examples and standard results
- Rings and Fields — definitions and standard results

## Unit 3 — Propositional Logic & FSM

- Propositional logic: proposition, first-order logic, basic logical operations, truth tables, tautologies, contradictions
- Algebra of proposition, logical implications, logical equivalence, predicates, normal forms, universal & existential quantifiers
- Introduction to finite state machines (FSM); FSM as models of physical systems; equivalence machines
- FSM as language recognizers

## Unit 4 — Graph Theory

- Introduction and basic terminology of graphs
- Planar graphs, multigraphs, weighted graphs, isomorphic graphs
- Paths, cycles, connectivity; shortest path in weighted graph
- Eulerian paths and circuits
- Hamiltonian paths and circuits; graph coloring; chromatic number
- Isomorphism and homomorphism of graphs

## Unit 5 — Posets, Hasse Diagram, and Lattices

- Ordered sets; Hasse diagrams of partially ordered sets
- Isomorphic ordered sets; well-ordered sets
- Properties of lattices; bounded and complemented lattices

## Unit 6 — Combinatorics

- Permutation and combination, binomial theorem, multinomial coefficients
- Recurrence relations and generating functions
- Recursive algorithms
- Linear recurrence relations with constant coefficients — homogeneous, particular, and total solutions
- Solving recurrence relations via generating functions
